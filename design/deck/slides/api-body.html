Example: a pick order

POST /v1/deliveries
{
  "message_id": "uuid",
  "external_ref": "0080012345",
  "warehouse": "BAL-WH01",
  "owner": "DEFAULT",
  "pick_mode": "single",
  "required_by": "2026-09-22",
  "allow_short": true,
  "ship_to": {{ "name": "Acme Auto Parts", "postcode": "3220" }},
  "lines": [{{ "delivery_line": 10, "sku": "ABC123",
    "batch": null, "qty": 10, "uom": "EA" }}]
}}

null batch

The WMS chooses by FIFO. Fill it in to force a batch.

pick_mode auto

The WMS groups small orders into a batch when three or more share a zone.

What comes back

202 with wms_id and what could and could not be allocated. Then delivery.allocated fires.
