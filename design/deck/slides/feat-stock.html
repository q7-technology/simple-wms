Features · 1 of 3

Platform and stock

Structure

Site → warehouse → zone → location. Pick sequence per location for walk order. Location type, access class, mixing rule, capacity.

Stock ledger

On hand, reserved, available per location and batch. FIFO by received date. Received date travels with transfers.

Products

Unit, decimals allowed, batch tracking switch, preferred zone, min/max at pick face, many barcodes per product.

Lookup

Where is it (by product), what is here (by shelf), across one or all warehouses. Same call feeds the scanner and the API.

Putaway rules

Same product with space → empty in preferred zone → any allowed empty → overflow and flag. Operator can override; ledger records the truth.

Master data

Locations and products editable on desktop or by CSV import with a preview that names problem rows before committing.
