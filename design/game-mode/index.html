A second look for Simple WMS: the manager runs the warehouse from a toy-town map, and every job opens as a panel over it. Scanner screens are the same jobs with bigger buttons. Each page opens at its designed size, and links between screens work.

These are static snapshots of the design, each in its starting state. The clicking, ticking and "Ding!" messages work in the live design canvas, not here. The original design in design/index.html is still what the app is built to.
